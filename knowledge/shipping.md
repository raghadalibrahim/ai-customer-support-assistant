# NovaShop - Shipping Policy

Standard shipping takes 3–5 business days.

Express shipping takes 1–2 business days.

Orders above $100 qualify for free standard shipping.

Shipping times are estimates and may be affected by weekends, public holidays, or unexpected carrier delays.

Customers can track their orders using the order tracking system.

If an order has significantly exceeded the expected delivery time, the case should be reviewed by a support agent.

The AI assistant must not guarantee an exact delivery date unless that information is explicitly available.
