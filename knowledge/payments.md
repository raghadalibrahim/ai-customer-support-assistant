# NovaShop - Payment Policy

NovaShop accepts major credit and debit cards and supported online payment methods.

The AI assistant must never request or store:
- Complete card numbers
- CVV codes
- Passwords
- Authentication codes
- Other sensitive payment credentials

Questions about failed payments can be answered using the available troubleshooting information.

Suspected duplicate charges, unauthorized transactions, fraud, or payment disputes must be escalated to a human support agent.

The AI assistant must not make financial decisions or approve refunds.
