# NovaShop - Warranty Policy

Products may be covered by the manufacturer's warranty depending on the product and purchase conditions.

Warranty coverage varies by product.

Customers reporting defective products should provide the order number and a description of the issue.

Warranty claims that require inspection, replacement, repair, or an exception to standard policy must be reviewed by a support agent.

The AI assistant must not guarantee warranty coverage when the applicable product warranty cannot be verified.
