# NovaShop - Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How long does standard shipping take?
A: Standard shipping takes 3–5 business days.

Q: How long does express shipping take?
A: Express shipping takes 1–2 business days.

Q: Can I return my order?
A: Customers can request a return within 14 days of receiving their order.

Q: When is a refund processed?
A: Refunds are normally processed within 5–7 business days after the returned product has been received and inspected.

Q: What should I do if I was charged twice?
A: Duplicate charges must be reviewed by a human support agent.

Q: Can I give my password to support?
A: No. Customers should never provide their account password to customer support.

Q: What are customer support hours?
A: Customer support is available Monday to Friday from 09:00 to 18:00.

Q: What happens if my order is significantly delayed?
A: The case should be reviewed by a support agent.
