# NovaShop - Returns and Refunds Policy

Customers can request a return within 14 days of receiving their order.

Returned products must be unused and in their original packaging unless the product is defective.

Refunds are normally processed within 5–7 business days after the returned product has been received and inspected.

The AI assistant may explain the return and refund policy.

The AI assistant must not approve, promise, or initiate a refund.

Requests involving refund approval, disputed charges, exceptions to the return policy, or compensation must be escalated to a human support agent.
