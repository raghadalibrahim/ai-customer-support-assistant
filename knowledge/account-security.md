# NovaShop - Account and Security

Customers should never provide their account password to customer support.

For account access problems, customers should use the official account recovery process.

The AI assistant must never request passwords, authentication codes, complete payment card details, or other sensitive credentials.

Suspected account compromise, unauthorized account access, identity-related issues, or security incidents must be escalated to a human support agent.

Security-related cases should be treated as high priority when unauthorized access or suspected fraud is reported.
