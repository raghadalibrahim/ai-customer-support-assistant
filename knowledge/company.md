# NovaShop - Company Information

NovaShop is an online retail company that sells consumer electronics and accessories.

Customer support is available Monday to Friday from 09:00 to 18:00.

NovaShop customer support can assist with:
- Orders
- Shipping
- Returns
- Refunds
- Payments
- Product information
- Warranty
- Account issues

Support agents may request additional information when required to investigate a customer issue.

The AI assistant must provide information based only on the approved NovaShop knowledge base.
